## Project Overview
VendoSync is a comprehensive management information system designed to centralize and streamline the operations of a Piso WiFi Vendo Internet Service Provider (ISP). The platform brings customer relationship management, contract administration, asset tracking, collection operations, financial monitoring, and technical support into a unified system.  

The system provides dedicated portals for administrators, collectors, and technicians, allowing each role to access the tools and information relevant to their responsibilities. By connecting field collection activities with centralized financial records, VendoSync helps improve operational visibility, accountability, and the management of distributed WiFi Vendo assets.  

The platform is deployed for M&L Internet Services as the **M&L ERP System, powered by VendoSync**.

## The Problem
Managing a Piso WiFi Vendo ISP involves coordinating multiple operational activities, including customer agreements, deployed machines, field collections, revenue sharing, customer payouts, operational expenses, and equipment maintenance.  

When these activities are handled through disconnected records and manual processes, it becomes more difficult to maintain accurate financial records, monitor collection progress, track machine assignments, and respond to technical issues in the field.  

VendoSync addresses these challenges by consolidating operational data and workflows into a centralized management system, connecting administrative oversight with day-to-day field operations.

### Objectives
* **Centralize ISP Operations:** Bring customer management, contracts, assets, collections, and support into one system. 
* **Improve Collection Accountability:** Provide collectors with a structured workflow for identifying vending machines, recording revenue, and tracking collection progress.  
* **Streamline Financial Management:** Track gross revenue, company shares, customer payables, payouts, and operational expenses. 
* **Improve Asset Visibility:** Maintain WiFi Vendo records, deployment information, assignments, and QR codes. 
* **Coordinate Technical Support:** Enable issues to be reported, prioritized, assigned to technicians, and tracked through support tickets. 
* **Strengthen Access Control:** Apply role-based permissions to separate administrative, collection, and technical responsibilities. 
* **Improve Operational Reporting:** Provide dashboards, financial metrics, and collection reports for monitoring business performance.

## Infrastructure and Technical Implementation
The system is built with Laravel and PHP on the backend, with Vue.js, TypeScript, Inertia.js, and Tailwind CSS powering the user interface. Wayfinder supports routing integration between the frontend and backend.  

Its modular structure separates customer management, asset administration, collections, financial operations, and technical support while connecting these workflows through shared business records. This allows the system to support both administrative management and mobile-friendly field collection activities.

### Outcome and Impact
* **Centralized Operations:** Customer records, contracts, assets, collections, financial transactions, expenses, and support tickets are managed through a unified system.
* **More Structured Field Collections:** QR-based machine identification and guided revenue recording help collectors associate transactions with the correct Vendo.
* **Improved Financial Visibility:** Revenue, company shares, customer payables, payouts, and operational expenses can be reviewed through dedicated modules and reports.
* **Connected Maintenance Workflow:** Field issues can be reported and tracked through support tickets assigned to technicians.
* **Role-Specific Workflows:** Administrators, collectors, and technicians have dedicated tools aligned with their operational responsibilities.
